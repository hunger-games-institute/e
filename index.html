A word to the Gamemaker

An arena is an expensive stage, and a Games with the wrong Victor is a poor evening for everyone, you included. The tribute who survives is rarely the one the audience came to see. The Bureau closes that gap.

Our District 3 engineers watch every tribute from the Reaping onward and present you one ranked list, updated live. You decide whether to follow it or to nudge the arena toward a better ending. The forecast serves your craft, and the craft stays yours.

The four instruments

Survival Index

Training scores, body composition, water and fire skill, and hours since the last sponsor gift.

Sponsor Appeal

Real-time gift volume and Capitol parlor sentiment. A Victor nobody funds makes for a quiet finale.

Arena Fit

Each tribute scored against your scheduled terrain, hazards, and weather. Swamp, glacier, or clock face: we hold priors for all.

Story Yield

Alliances, rivalries, and betrayals. We flag the moment a tribute becomes a symbol, so you can decide what to do with it.
