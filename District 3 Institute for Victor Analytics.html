How a Gamemaker chooses a winner with us

A Games is a story with a deadline. The Institute turns an arena into a dashboard so the Head Gamemaker can steer toward the ending the Capitol will remember, and the tribute most likely to deliver it.

Survival Index

Combines training scores, body composition, water and fire skill, and how long a tribute has gone without sponsor gifts.

Audience Heat

Live sentiment from Capitol viewing parlors and District squares. A winner nobody cheers for is a failed Games.

Arena Fit

Matches each tribute against the terrain, hazards, and weather you've scheduled. Swamp, glacier, or clock face: we have priors for all.

Narrative Yield

Scores alliances, rivalries, and betrayals. Our model flags the moment a tribute becomes a symbol, so you can act before the crowd does.
